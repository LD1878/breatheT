Privacy notice

Breathe Therm Ltd (“we”) uses the details you send us only to answer your enquiry and, if you ask us to, to arrange a survey or send a technical pack.

What we collect

Enquiry forms ask for your name, email, phone number and postcode, who you are (owner, architect or installer), and anything you choose to write about the building. The house quiz also stores the answers you pick. Calling or emailing us gives us whatever you say in that call or message.

Why we hold it

We use it to reply, to prepare for a visit, and to send the documents you asked for. That is our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry you started. We do not use it for marketing lists, and we do not sell it.

Who sees it

The enquiry comes to hello@breathetherm.co.uk. If your email app sends it, it travels as an ordinary email from you to us. If the form sends it for you, it goes through Web3Forms, who pass the message on and do not keep it for their own use. We do not pass the details to anyone else unless you ask us to involve an installer or a surveyor.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as the conversation is active, and for up to two years after the last reply, so we can pick up a project that was paused. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@breathetherm.co.uk or call 01285 407088. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you think we have handled your details badly.
