Why now

5.9 million pre‑1919 buildings in the UK cannot be insulated with modern foam without rotting from the inside.

BreatheTherm supplies a breathable, mineral-based insulation system, proven for 30+ years across continental Europe, built specifically for solid-wall heritage properties, alongside Quote2Certify, a case-management platform that takes every job from quote to certificate.

Business Summary

BreatheTherm supplies breathable, mineral-based wall insulation and a certified installer network for owners of pre‑1919 solid-wall buildings. Our clients are homeowners, architects and local authorities who need to cut heating bills and meet retrofit obligations without destroying the fabric of the building. Unlike foam and plastic insulation incumbents, which trap moisture and cause decay in 98% of heritage applications, our clients get proven German-standard (WTA) breathable performance, backed by a digital certificate of compliance. We already have four live installation jobs, a Building Supplies partnership in discussion, and Cotswold District Council piloting interest.

Customer Problem

Owners of historic buildings face a lose-lose choice: leave solid walls uninsulated and pay for it in heating bills, or insulate with modern foam and risk trapped moisture, black mould, and structural decay. There is no reliable mechanism ensuring the right breathable material is specified and installed correctly by a skilled trade.

Our Solution

A five-component "mix-and-match" silicate and perlite insulation system that mimics the original building's physics: breathable, fire‑resistant, and WTA‑certified. Paired with Quote2Certify, an AI-assisted app that triages the job by photo, guides installers step-by-step, and issues a compliance certificate that adds resale value to the property.

Market Opportunity

~5.9 million buildings in the UK were constructed before 1919. At an annual retrofit rate of 1–2% of heritage stock, that's roughly 100,000 projects a year, an addressable market of approximately £1.5bn annually at £15k per project. Our beachhead is the Cotswolds and South-West heritage belt, where founder Josh Tetley has 25 years of renovation relationships.

Traction

Four live installation jobs totalling c.£44,000 in invoiced work (two paid, one in progress); early-stage partnership discussions with Proctor (Building Supplies) to list BreatheTherm in their product portfolio; Cotswold District Council exploring a pilot for local‑authority‑owned heritage stock; architects requesting BreatheTherm-led CPD training; and over 100 qualified connections made at the Listed Buildings Show.

Business Model

Materials (transactional): supply to certified installers at c.£8k average order value and 50% margin, targeting 30 partners × 10 jobs per year.

Quote2Certify (recurring SaaS): licensed case-management platform (quote → contract → install → sign-off → certificate), licensed to installers, tradesmen and contractors beyond our own network.
